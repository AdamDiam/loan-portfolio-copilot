## Failures
### q23: How many loans became non-performing in each quarter of 2025?

```sql
SELECT strftime('%Y', default_date) || '-Q' || ((CAST(strftime('%m', default_date) AS INTEGER) + 2) / 3) AS quarter,
       COUNT(*) AS npe_loan_count
FROM loans
WHERE default_date >= '2025-01-01'
  AND default_date < '2026-01-01'
  AND is_npe = 1
GROUP BY quarter
ORDER BY quarter
```
